StikBot Sticker Maker (the "app") is published by [LEGAL NAME OF THE MADBOT COMPANY] ("we", "us"), South Africa. This policy explains what information the Android app and the Windows desktop app collect, why, and your choices. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • Your photos and stickers stay on your device. We never upload them to our servers.
  • Removing a photo's background happens on your device.
  • We only learn who you are if you buy the unlock or sign in on the desktop app, and then only your Google account email and name.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are removed when you unlock the app.
  • We do not sell your information.

What stays on your device

The pictures you pick, the stickers you make and your sticker packs are stored in the app's own storage on your phone or computer. The app reads a picture only when you choose it or share it to the app. When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", the stickers are handed to WhatsApp on the same device through Android's standard sticker mechanism. We do not receive them.

Who else handles information

  • Google handles sign-in, Google Play purchases and, in the free version, advertising (Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform consent form). Google's own privacy policy applies to what it collects: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Our licence server (operated by us in South Africa) stores the account and purchase details listed above.
  • Google Play services provides the on-device model used for background removal. The image is processed on your device.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Ads and your choices

The free version shows banner ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). Unlocking the app removes ads.

How long we keep information

We keep account and licence records for as long as your licence exists, and for as long afterwards as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute purposes. Check-in records (computer name, time and IP address) are kept with the licence.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. Deleting your licence record means we can no longer restore your unlock for you. If you are in South Africa you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including to complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR rights apply to you as well.

In the desktop app, "Sign out" removes the licence from that computer and frees it for another one.

Security

Communication with our licence server uses HTTPS. Licence tokens are digitally signed. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is little to lose.
